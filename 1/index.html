Meet Your Client

We help furniture retailers and manufacturers create, visualize and configure products more easily and at scale. PyxMagic Pro transforms ordinary iPhone product capture into complete ecommerce-ready imagery, including professional silhouettes, lifestyle scenes, dimensioned images and high-resolution assets. PyxMagic Draping presents furniture dynamically in different fabrics, leathers and finishes, while our Sectional Configurator lets shoppers and sales associates build photoreal sectional arrangements with accurate views, dimensions and tear sheets.

002
Refine Concepts

I worked in Illustrator to showcase the functionality of the app in an easy to understand way. I started with an image of a piece of furniture and then worked around it to illustrate a realistic scene that shows how the app actually isolates furniture.

I was tasked with creating a t-shirt design for an upcoming conference for PyxMagic to showcase their product. My idea was to use the two main colors of the palette to create a high contrast, eye-catching design that showcases the functionality of the product. I started with simple blocks and then slowly added relevant elements pertaining to the product. I then added half-tone, sunray and typographical flourishes to enhance the design.
