Project Brief

I was tasked with creating a watchface for the Apple Watch. I created every single element from scratch in Illustrator, including the typeface, and then created a realistic mockup animation in After Effects.

002
Refine Concepts

For the typeface, I started with simple block lettering and then added 3D elements to create depth and dimension. For the background of the typeface I played around with different 3D tools in Illustrator until I created 3 cohesive, but visually distinct designs.

Once I finished both elements I put the type on the patterned background and varied the color of the type to appropriately match with the background.

To finalize the design, I used a clipping mask of the patterned designs over the clock-face element of the watch, and then decided to go with just black/white for the background and readable elements to contrast the fun and colorful design of the actual moving watch face.
